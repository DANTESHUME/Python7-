<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Neo-Brutalism / Ubiquitous Computing
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Raw Functionality**: Expose the structure. Borders are thick, colors are high-contrast, no soft shadows.
  2. **Information Density**: Use monospaced fonts and grid lines to mimic a terminal or code editor environment.
  3. **Directness**: Interactions are instant and clicky. No smooth fades; things toggle on/off.
  4. **Honesty**: The UI doesn't pretend to be a physical object. It's a digital tool.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **High Contrast**: Black (#000000) and White (#FFFFFF) base.
  - **Accents**: Pure RGB colors (Red #FF0000, Blue #0000FF, Green #00FF00) used for status (Error, Info, Success).
  - **Intent**: To evoke the feeling of a raw Python console or a developer tool. Serious but playful in its boldness.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Modular Grid**: The screen is divided into strict rectangular regions separated by thick black borders (2px-4px).
  - **Sidebar Navigation**: A persistent, collapsible sidebar for the 7-day timeline, resembling a file explorer.
  - **Split Screen**: Content on the left, interactive code/terminal on the right.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Hard Shadows**: Buttons have solid black drop shadows at 45 degrees (no blur).
  - **Marquee Text**: For announcements or completion celebrations.
  - **Terminal-style Inputs**: Input fields look like command prompts (`>>>`).

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Tactile Feedback**: Hover states invert colors immediately. Active states shift the element down to "press" it.
  - **Gamification**: Progress is tracked via a "system status" panel that updates in real-time.

  **Animation**:
  - **Step-based**: Animations are frame-by-frame or instant (0s duration), avoiding ease-in-out curves.
  - **Glitch Effects**: Subtle glitching on success/failure states to fit the "coding" theme.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Primary**: `JetBrains Mono` or `Courier Prime` (Monospace) for EVERYTHING, including headings.
  - **Hierarchy**: Size and weight (Bold/Regular) distinguish sections. Uppercase for labels.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Glassmorphism / Ethereal Tech
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Transparency & Depth**: Layers of frosted glass to create hierarchy.
  2. **Soft Light**: Backgrounds are deep, moving gradients that simulate an aurora or nebula.
  3. **Fluidity**: Everything floats and flows. No hard edges.
  4. **Focus**: The active content is clear; inactive content blurs into the background.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Dark Mode Base**: Deep midnight blues and purples.
  - **Gradients**: Cyan, Magenta, and Violet gradients for the "AI" feel.
  - **Intent**: To represent the "magic" of AI and Python. It feels futuristic and intelligent.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Floating Cards**: Content lives on translucent cards centered in the viewport.
  - **Z-Axis Navigation**: Instead of scrolling down, users feel like they are moving "deeper" into the content.
  - **Organic Shapes**: Background blobs that morph slowly.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Frosted Glass**: `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` on all containers.
  - **Glowing Borders**: 1px borders with a gradient stroke.
  - **Neon Accents**: Text and icons that seem to emit light.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Hover Glow**: Elements light up when interacted with.
  - **Smooth Transitions**: Long duration (500ms+) ease-in-out transitions.

  **Animation**:
  - **Parallax**: Background moves slower than foreground.
  - **Breathing**: Cards have a subtle vertical float animation.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: `Inter` or `Space Grotesk` (Clean Sans-serif), tracking tight.
  - **Body**: `Inter` with high readability.
  - **Code**: `Fira Code` with ligatures.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Swiss Style / International Typographic Style (Digital Adaptation)
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Grid Systems**: Mathematical alignment of all elements.
  2. **Asymmetry**: Dynamic balance instead of centered symmetry.
  3. **Clarity**: Content is king. Decoration is stripped away.
  4. **Typography as Image**: Large, bold type is the primary visual element.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Neutral Base**: Off-white (#F5F5F5) or Light Gray.
  - **Primary**: Swiss Red (#FF3B30) for emphasis.
  - **Secondary**: Black (#1D1D1F) for text.
  - **Intent**: Professional, academic, and clean. It treats the learning material with respect.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Asymmetric Grid**: Content column is offset. Large whitespace on the left or right.
  - **Horizontal Rhythm**: Strong horizontal lines separating sections.
  - **Sticky Headers**: Section titles stick while content scrolls.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Huge Typography**: Day numbers (e.g., "01") are massive and cropped.
  - **Micro-interactions**: Subtle color shifts, no movement.
  - **Geometric Icons**: Simple shapes (circles, squares, triangles).

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Snap Scroll**: Sections snap into place.
  - **Hover Underlines**: Links get a thick, colored underline on hover.

  **Animation**:
  - **Slide & Reveal**: Content slides out from behind masks.
  - **Staggered Text**: Text lines appear one by one.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Font**: `Helvetica Now` or `Arial` (Neo-grotesque).
  - **Rules**: One font family, varying weights (Black, Bold, Regular). Tight leading for headings.
</idea>
